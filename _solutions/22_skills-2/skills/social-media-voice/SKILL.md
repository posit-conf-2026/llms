# Social media voice

Write like a neighbor who loves movies, not like a brand.
Warm, specific, plainspoken. A little playful is fine.

Lead with the movie, not the store.
Name a real title whenever one is relevant.

Posts are short: one or two sentences, and end with an invitation to visit.
Sign-off is the store name only — no team names, no hashtags.

Never promise discounts, availability, or dates that are not confirmed
in a store file first.

# Store policies

The 2026 renewal campaign date is September 1, 2026.

A member is lapsed when they have no rental in the last 90 days and no dues payment in the last 12 months.
A rental 90 days old or newer counts as recent.
A dues payment from September 1, 2025 or later counts as recent.

A member with an unreturned rental is never lapsed.
Resolve the rental before you contact that member.

Mark statuses in new files and never delete store rows.
Members without an email still belong on the win-back list because the manager calls them.
Order the win-back list by tier, with Founders first, Family second, and Basic third.
Within each tier, put the member with the most recent activity first.

When you mention the former no-late-fees program, include the due date, the grace period, and the restocking fee.
The store must not imply that a late return has no cost.

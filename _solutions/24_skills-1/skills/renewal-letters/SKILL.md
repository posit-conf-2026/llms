# Renewal letters

Write with the voice of a neighbor who knows the store and wants the member back.
Be warm, specific, and plainspoken.
Do not sound like a mass promotion.

Save each letter as a Markdown file in `letters/drafts/`.
Use the member's name in the filename with lowercase words separated by hyphens.

Each letter includes:

1. A personal greeting.
2. A reference to the member's last rented title.
3. One renewal incentive that matches the member's tier.
4. A clear invitation to visit or call the store.
5. A brief sign-off from the Last Blockbuster team.

Offer Basic members a free-rental code.
Offer Family members a family-night bundle.
Offer Founders members a commemorative card.

Do not promise an unlisted discount, a title's availability, or any no-late-fees terms.
Do not mention overdue fees unless the task asks for them.
